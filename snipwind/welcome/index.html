Good to know

It does not work on some pages

Chrome blocks every extension on its own pages, such as the Chrome Web Store, settings and new-tab pages. Snipwind works on ordinary websites.

The shortcut does nothing

Another extension may already use it. You can change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
